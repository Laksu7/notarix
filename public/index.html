What NOTARIX does

A single system for the law office's notarization logs — instead of handwritten record books.

1. Digital Record Entry

Record notarized documents after signing — document number, type, client name, date, status, and location.

2. Duplicate Checking

The system checks whether a document number already exists before saving — duplicates cannot be recorded.

3. Organized Records

Every record is stored in one organized database and displayed in a clean, paginated table.

4. Search & Retrieval

Find any record by document number, document type, client name, or date — in seconds.

5. Update / Edit

Authorized staff can correct miswritten entries or update record information safely.

6. Status & Location

Every document is either For Submission (at the Law Office) or Submitted (at the Main Office) — pick the status and the location updates automatically. Select many and update them in one Bulk Update.

7. Monthly Reports

Records are organized by month, with export to PDF or Excel for the monthly report.

8. Roles

The administrator manages staff accounts and settings; secretaries handle daily records.
